## Adaptation

The downloaded model depicts the regular 2024 U9. For the hidden U9 Xtreme simulator slot, the runtime aligns and scales the exterior to the Xtreme's published dimensions, separates wheel geometry into four physics-driven pivots, omits the unseen interior behind opaque glazing, and adds original procedural aero details and brake illumination. The visual remains an interpretation rather than an official Xtreme model. The source GLB is preserved so the changes can be inspected and replaced later.

The physical dimensions, mass, and power/speed targets come from [BYD's U9 Xtreme technical specifications](https://media.byd.com/focus-technique-de-la-yangwang-u9-a-la-yangwang-u9-xtreme-detentrice-du-record-mondial-de-vitesse/?lang=fra) and [503.17 km/h announcement](https://media.byd.com/the-fastest-gets-faster-yangwang-u9-xtreme-tops-500kmh-to-set-new-global-speed-record-in-abu-dhabi/?lang=eng). Drive force and grip are game tuning.

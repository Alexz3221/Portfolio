Yangwang U9 Xtreme

The key-6 car uses “2024 BYD Yangwang U9” by Ddiaz Design, also shared by OUTPISTON, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The simulator uniformly scales the original mesh and separates its wheels for animation; the original body, interior, proportions, and materials are retained. The source uploader says the mesh is based on a Need for Speed Mobile model; see the provenance and license notes. This is the regular U9 appearance, not an official Xtreme mesh.

The key-6 driving setup uses BYD's U9 Xtreme technical specifications and speed announcement. Drive force and grip are game tuning. A lightweight procedural visual is the loading and failure fallback.

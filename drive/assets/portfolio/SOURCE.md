# Portfolio City asset sources

The environment uses a selection of models by Quaternius under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The GLBs come from the creator’s [Cyberpunk Game Kit](https://quaternius.com/packs/cyberpunkgamekit.html) and [Modular Sushi Restaurant Kit](https://poly.pizza/bundle/Modular-Sushi-Restaurant-Kit-LJZrZsNPM7).

The site scales the models to the street and lights their existing screens and signage. The sign kit’s original PNG atlas was reduced to 384 × 384 pixels for the web. The remaining models are direct GLB downloads.

`asphalt-diffuse.jpg` is derived from [Asphalt Track by Dimitrios Savva](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_track) under [Poly Haven’s CC0 asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license). The original 1K diffuse was resized to a 512 × 512 sRGB JPEG. Each tile represents 2 metres of asphalt.

`concrete-bump.jpg` is derived from [Concrete Wall 007 by Charlotte Baglioni, Dario Barresi, and Rico Cilliers](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_007) under the same license. The original 1K displacement map was resized to a 512 × 512 grayscale JPEG for linear bump mapping.

# Portfolio display art and surface textures

`display.json` is an uppercase and digit subset of Barlow Condensed Bold by Jeremy Tribby. The outlines were converted to Three.js typeface format and renamed Portfolio Display. The [original font and license](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed) are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. See `OFL.txt`.

`district-display.webp` was made with the built-in ImageGen tool. The image was encoded as WebP at 1024 pixels wide for the game.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape illustration for an LED billboard in a browser game's original cyberpunk city. Create a striking graphic illustration of three translucent architectural rings orbiting a dark suspended crystal data core above a night-time transit concourse, like an abstract promotional screen for a futuristic district. Deep ink-black background, hot vermilion and coral as the main colors, electric violet interference and tiny icy white highlights. Editorial graphic design with sharp layered print textures, halftone lines, high-contrast silhouettes and signal distortion contained in the composition. One clear central focal shape that reads from across a street. Leave the outer 8 percent dark for cropping and use a wide cinematic composition. Fill the frame with the billboard art itself, with no physical frame or environment mockup. No words, characters, brand logos, watermark, or reference to an existing game.

## Surface textures

The three wall textures were created with the built-in ImageGen tool and encoded as 768 × 768 WebP tiles. Portfolio City lights these color maps in the scene.

### `board-concrete.webp`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: repeating albedo texture for a browser 3D game in a dense near-future city. Create a square perfectly seamless edge-to-edge tile of aging exposed board-formed concrete at the base of a high-rise. Flat straight-on orthographic scan of the bare wall surface, with worn gray-brown cement, pores and small aggregate, irregular formwork grain, a few faint mineral streaks, occasional fastener holes and scuffed patches. The whole frame is material with an even distribution. Moderate texture contrast, good readable broad and small detail, muted desaturated neutral stone palette. Uniform diffuse illumination for an albedo map. Match opposite edges seamlessly. No view of a room or street, perspective, framing edges, drop shadows, dark corners, pools of light, writing, decals, cast shadow or watermark.

### `service-panels.webp`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: repeating albedo texture for a browser 3D game in a dense near-future city. Create a square, perfectly seamless tile of battered industrial service panels for the lower levels of a city megastructure. Flat orthographic straight-on scan, four equal broad blue-gray painted steel maintenance plates in a neat two-by-two grid with quiet thin aligned grooves, occasional flush screws, shallow scuffs, dirty rain runnels and sparse chipped paint on the edges. A few mismatched gray repair patches. Low to medium contrast with readable material wear and no dominant single stain. Match opposite edges and align the center seam with equally thin seams wrapping at the tile boundaries. Muted stormy charcoal and dusty steel blue, evenly lit diffuse albedo with zero lighting gradient. Full bleed, no borders or separate background. No text, numbers, caution stripes, brand marks, perspective, cast shadows, specular reflections, or illustrations.

### `market-tile.webp`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: repeating albedo texture for a browser 3D game in a dense near-future city. Square perfectly seamless texture of stained rectangular ceramic tile straight on, the facade of a decades-old underground market entrance near the street. Small subway-format tiles in even running bond, around eight rows tall and around five tiles wide. Muted dingy desaturated bone, soot gray, subtle old olive, with a few replacement tiles very slightly lighter. Uneven three-decade accumulation of faint grime in grout, subtle mineral stains, small glaze chips and rough speckling. Tone and scale consistent across the image. Match top/bottom and left/right grout and colors seamlessly. Flat orthographic close-up material scan fills the frame. Even diffuse light and medium-low contrast. No highlights or directional shadows baked into the image, no floor horizon, loose pieces, lettering, labels or framing.

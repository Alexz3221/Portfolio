## Simulator placement

The downloaded model depicts the regular 2024 U9. The runtime uniformly scales the complete model to a driveable size, centers it between its axles, and separates the original wheel geometry into four physics-driven pivots. It keeps the original body shape, interior, materials, glazing, and exterior details. No Xtreme-style bodywork or lighting is added. The source GLB is preserved so the placement can be inspected or replaced later.

The driving setup's mass, power, and speed targets come from [BYD's U9 Xtreme technical specifications](https://media.byd.com/focus-technique-de-la-yangwang-u9-a-la-yangwang-u9-xtreme-detentrice-du-record-mondial-de-vitesse/?lang=fra) and [503.17 km/h announcement](https://media.byd.com/the-fastest-gets-faster-yangwang-u9-xtreme-tops-500kmh-to-set-new-global-speed-record-in-abu-dhabi/?lang=eng). Drive force and grip are game tuning; the visual dimensions remain those of the supplied base U9 model.

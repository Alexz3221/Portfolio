01 / INTERACTIVE FICTION

Adventure
Studio.

A choose your own adventure app with a local Qwen story model. Stories keep track of characters, locations, inventory, facts, and unresolved threads so choices can build on what happened earlier.

  • Choose actions and edit generated prose
  • Rewind and replay from an earlier turn
  • Illustrate scenes with a local image model
  • Save or export a campaign

Runs locally; public demo and source release are future decisions.

02 / IMAGE PLAYGROUND

Image
Studio.

An image workspace inside Adventure Studio. It combines model selection, styles, prompt composition, image analysis, and generated variations in one place.

  • Build prompts from text or an analyzed image
  • Compare styles and aspect ratios
  • Generate variations and browse a local gallery

The original image files and models stay on the workstation.

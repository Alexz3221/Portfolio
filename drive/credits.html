Yangwang U9 Xtreme

The key-6 car adapts “2024 BYD Yangwang U9” by Ddiaz Design, also shared by OUTPISTON, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Changes: scale and orientation for the simulator, separate wheel pivots, and add Xtreme-inspired aero and brake illumination. The source uploader says the mesh is based on a Need for Speed Mobile model; see the provenance and license notes. This is an adapted U9 visual, not an official Xtreme mesh.

Its dimensions, mass and performance target follow BYD's technical specifications and speed announcement. Drive force and grip are game tuning. The original lightweight procedural visual is the loading and failure fallback.

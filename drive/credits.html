Portfolio City

Street props by Quaternius include models from the Cyberpunk Game Kit. The asphalt color and concrete bump maps come from Poly Haven. These models and textures are CC0. See the asset sources and the display art, wall textures and type credits.

The Comet Night edition adapts Cyberpunk car by 4d_Bob under CC BY 4.0. The adaptation fits the Comet chassis, animates its wheels and reuses the original light artwork. See the model source and adaptation details.

The metal fire escapes adapt Modular Fire Escape by Juniix (CC0). Parked vehicles adapt Cyberpunk Car by SamTheCaribbean and Sci-Fi Vehicle 007 by Unity Fan (credited under CC BY 4.0). See the source and adaptation notes.

Yangwang U9 Xtreme

The key-6 car uses “2024 BYD Yangwang U9” by Ddiaz Design, also shared by OUTPISTON, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The simulator uniformly scales the original mesh and separates its wheels for animation; the original body, interior, proportions, and materials are retained. The source uploader says the mesh is based on a Need for Speed Mobile model; see the provenance and license notes. This is the regular U9 appearance, not an official Xtreme mesh.

The key-6 driving setup uses BYD's U9 Xtreme technical specifications and speed announcement. Drive force and grip are game tuning. A lightweight procedural visual is the loading and failure fallback.
